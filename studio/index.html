Workspace tool

PDF Converter

Turn selectable PDF text into a chapter-based ZBOOK document.

PDF → ZBOOK

Convert a PDF

Each PDF page becomes a ZBOOK chapter. Scanned/image-only PDFs may produce little or no text. OCR and original page layout preservation are not included.

PDF conversion loads Mozilla PDF.js from a public CDN and requires an internet connection.
